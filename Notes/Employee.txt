============================================================
2. EMPLOYEES WHO LIVE IN MUMBAI
============================================================

Question:
Display details of employees who live in Mumbai.

db.Employee.find({city: "Mumbai"})

Cleaner:

db.Employee.find({city: "Mumbai"}).pretty()

============================================================
3. EMPLOYEE STAYING IN KURLA AND SALARY > 19000
============================================================

Question:
Display details of employee staying in Kurla and salary greater than 19000.

db.Employee.find({
    city: "Kurla",
    salary: {$gt: 19000}
})

Cleaner:

db.Employee.find({
    city: "Kurla",
    salary: {$gt: 19000}
}).pretty()

$gt = greater than

Therefore salary: {$gt: 19000} means salary > 19000.

============================================================
4. SALARY >= 10000 AND SALARY < 20000
============================================================

Question:
Display details of the employee whose salary is greater than equal 10000 but less than 20000.

db.Employee.find({
    salary: {$gte: 10000, $lt: 20000}
})

Cleaner:

db.Employee.find({
    salary: {$gte: 10000, $lt: 20000}
}).pretty()

$gte = greater than or equal to
$lt  = less than

Therefore:
10000 <= salary < 20000

============================================================
5. THANE OR MUMBAI AND SALARY > 10000
============================================================

Question:
Display details of employees staying in either Thane or Mumbai and salary greater than 10000.

db.Employee.find({
    $or: [
        {city: "Thane"},
        {city: "Mumbai"}
    ],
    salary: {$gt: 10000}
})

Cleaner:

db.Employee.find({
    $or: [
        {city: "Thane"},
        {city: "Mumbai"}
    ],
    salary: {$gt: 10000}
}).pretty()

Meaning:
(city = Thane OR city = Mumbai)
AND
salary > 10000

============================================================
6. SALARY != 10000 AND DEVELOPER OR TESTER
============================================================

Question:
Display details of employee whose salary is not equal to 10000 and working in either developer or tester department.

db.Employee.find({
    salary: {$ne: 10000},
    $or: [
        {department: "Developer"},
        {department: "Tester"}
    ]
})

Cleaner:

db.Employee.find({
    salary: {$ne: 10000},
    $or: [
        {department: "Developer"},
        {department: "Tester"}
    ]
}).pretty()

$ne = not equal to

Overall:
salary != 10000
AND
(department = Developer OR department = Tester)

============================================================
OPERATORS TO MEMORIZE
============================================================

$gt   = greater than
$gte  = greater than or equal to
$lt   = less than
$ne   = not equal to
$or   = either condition

============================================================
EXECUTION ORDER
============================================================

1. Start MongoDB server (mongod) if required by your college VM.
2. Open the MongoDB shell used by your college.
3. use EmployeeDB
4. db.createCollection("Employee")
5. Insert the documents.
6. db.Employee.find().pretty()
7. Execute Questions 2–6 one by one.

NOTE:
The exact command used to start the MongoDB server/shell can differ between MongoDB versions and college VM setups. The six Employee query commands above are independent of that startup method.
